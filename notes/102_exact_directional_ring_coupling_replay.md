# Exact directional ring coupling replay

Directional ring coupling terms are now closed proof objects rather than digest
carriers. Structural validation owns their exact dataclass path and field set.
Facts-bound replay reconstructs every committed identity from the current
branch: graph event, DOUBLE non-bridge bond, marker manifest, writer and ring
states, residual snapshots, stereo lifecycle, residual work, and the optional
closed-closure record.

Global replay sets prove that referenced work succeeded. Branch-local indexes
separately prove ownership, so a digest borrowed from another branch cannot
upgrade the coupling. Opening terms require no closed record; paired terms bind
the unique matching successor closure.

This is validation hardening only. Schema remains v8 and runtime chemistry is
unchanged.
