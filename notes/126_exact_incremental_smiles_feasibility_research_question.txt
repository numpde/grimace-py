Exact online SMILES generation without repeated future-execution search
Research proposal and request for expert critique — 2026-10-16

We seek an exact online generator for every standard SMILES representation
of a fixed molecular graph. At each step it must return precisely the local
text emissions that still admit a complete valid serialization, together
with their distinct labelled successor states. The central question is
whether exact joint feasibility can be maintained under actual writer-state
updates, with controlled work, without searching complete hypothetical
writer executions for each candidate. Bounded compositional summaries over
physical graph regions are one proposed route, not the definition of success.

The requested result is a constructive theorem with explicit parameter and
cost bounds, or a counterexample or lower bound identifying which proposed
restriction makes that theorem impossible. This proposal is not a claim that
such a theorem already holds. Correctness, effective construction, compact
representation, and practical update costs are separate questions. An
alternative architecture satisfying the same behavioral and work requirements
is equally relevant. Failure of the separator route would not by itself
disprove the overall vision.

Intended readers: researchers in graph algorithms, dynamic constraint
satisfaction, automata/grammar theory, enumeration, and chemical informatics.

1. Target language and molecular identity

Let G=(V,E,F) be a finite immutable labelled molecular graph. Vertex and edge
identifiers distinguish physical objects; F records element, isotope,
charge, hydrogen inventory, numeric atom class, bonding, aromatic facts,
and specified or unspecified stereochemistry. Labels are operational
identities, not extra characters that must appear in the SMILES. No quotient
by graph automorphisms is taken inside the walker.

Let O be the syntax of OpenSMILES v1.0 [1]. Use end-of-string as its
terminator; transport whitespace and trailing record
metadata are outside this proposal. Fix an explicit semantic interpretation
I for matters requiring further precision. Define

  L_I(G) = { s in O : there exists a fact-preserving bijection
                     from the graph interpreted by I(s) to G }.

Semantic failure makes s absent from L_I(G). The identity relation below is
part of the model; different chemical equivalence conventions can define
different languages even when they share the same string grammar.

The bijection preserves graph vertices, their fields, edges, hydrogen
inventory, and stereo assignment. Explicit graph hydrogens remain vertices;
non-graph hydrogen counts remain counts. Aromatic/Kekule equivalence requires
an explicit bonding-and-valence relation. It is not permission to accept
whatever a particular toolkit normalizes. Unspecified stereo must not become
specified. Isotope absence differs from isotope zero; numeric atom-class
zero can have omitted or explicit spelling. Labelled executions retain the
chosen bijection even when two executions emit identical text.

OpenSMILES permits ring labels 0–99, matching numerically and reusable after
closure; a label is one digit or '%' followed by two digits. Dot-separated
segments can reconnect through ring bonds. Its branch grammar includes a
leading dot. Its stereo repertoire exceeds tetrahedral and ordinary alkene
stereo. These are obligations of the standard target [1, Formal Grammar;
Reading SMILES: Rings, Disconnected Structures, and Chirality]. RDKit writer
support is a separate, version-specific language; RDKit parser acceptance
proves neither this language nor that writer's exact support.

The project's MAXIMAL contract means every and only string in L_I(G), not
the current implemented subset. The present interpretation allows unbounded
leading zeros in numeric isotope/class fields and specifies aromatic
hydrogen conventions where the reference is underspecified [2]. Experts
should review those choices; they must be explicit hypotheses of a theorem.
Consequently L_I(G) can be infinite for a finite graph. A finite enumeration
of examples cannot define its coverage.

For an initial theorem, parameterize a finite fact-domain signature D and
its constraint relations, rather than silently treating today's chemistry
implementation as the full standard. D must identify supported valence,
aromaticity, stereo classes, constraint arities, and lexical residual types.
Let b bound numeric input bit lengths when a finite-value parameter is needed;
otherwise state their representation and costs explicitly. Full coverage
requires an interpretation and treatment for every standard construct, including
wildcards, quadruple bonding, extended and non-tetrahedral stereo, and mixed
aromatic spellings. A theorem for a restricted D is valuable but remains
restricted. Nonstandard toolkit extensions are deliberately outside O;
missing standard constructs are unfinished obligations, not extensions.

2. Actual states, local steps, and productivity

A state q contains only actual commitments: emitted/visited graph objects,
current owner and grammar phase, pending atom/bond/ring lexical residuals,
open ring identities and endpoint obligations, actual saved branch frames,
and remaining domains of joint chemical, ordering, and stereo constraints.
The immutable graph is available throughout. A saved frame identifies its
real return owner and permitted residual actions. The stack can have O(|V|)
frames; bounded message size must not be confused with bounded total state.
Future traversal ranks, chosen future entries, and future branch-end indices
are not part of this proposed representation.

Fix a finite-branching, effectively computable local transition relation

  q --a/t--> q',

where a identifies the labelled local decision and t its emitted text. Its
event scheme includes atom entry/completion, bond and ring
operations, branch entry/return, dot-segment starts, and lexical emissions.
Its adequacy is a proof obligation, not an assumption that the present
implementation exhausts the grammar. Local admissibility must be defined
without invoking Live: it applies syntax rules and consequences of actual
commitments, rather than calling the completion decision under study.
A public choice is (t,q'), with one emitted character t and a successor
q' consistent with the actual commitments. Distinct
labelled successors survive even if their t values agree. Redundant
bookkeeping paths to the same actual (t,q') need not create multiplicity.
Choosing text retains all matching successors; it does not determinize them
prematurely.

The principal research question fixes single-character public emissions,
retaining partial atoms, numbers, ring labels, and stereo markers in q.
This fixes the output granularity before analyzing work; replacing a local
choice by a whole future serialization would change the problem. Larger-token
variants require separate delay and cost results, not just language equality.
Finite branching must hold even when lexical loops produce arbitrarily long spellings.
Existence of a finite completion, rather than eventual termination along
every infinite sequence of choices, defines productivity.

Let Accept(q) mean that grammar and lexical obligations are closed, every
physical graph object is accounted for, ring pairs are discharged, and all
facts/stereo agree with G. Let Live(q) mean that some finite legal execution
from q reaches Accept. Let Cand_G(q) be the locally admissible public
transitions. Required output is

  Choices_G(q) = { (t,q') : (q --a/t--> q') in Cand_G(q)
                            and Live(q') }.

For every reachable public q, both inclusions are obligations: publish no
dead successor and omit no productive local successor. Accepted labelled
paths must project onto exactly L_I(G), realizing each qualifying graph
bijection. Correctness of completed strings alone does not establish exact
productivity of partial states.

Acceptance is reported separately as an end-of-string option. If internal
epsilon bookkeeping is used, define a public step as epsilon* / nonempty
emission / epsilon*, preserving every distinct labelled result. Epsilon
closure must be effectively finite or have a proved terminating quotient; epsilon cycles
cannot conceal a future search. Physical decisions without text remain
visible in the resulting state. Their event-to-grammar correspondence must
be included in soundness and completeness proofs.

3. The architectural question and proposed finite-interface route

Exact joint relations may retain unresolved alternatives. Separate feasible
traversal, capacity, and stereo marginals do not establish a jointly feasible
serialization. The intended representation owns immutable physical facts,
actual commitments, and their remaining constraints. A cached future-state
tree, a relation indexed by all future visited-atom subsets, or future
traversal schedules under renamed variables would not meet the goal.

This restriction needs mathematical substance. A proposed construction must
identify every stored index and bound its cardinality, describe the update
operators, and bound their loops. Avoiding literal suffix strings is not a
work bound. Finite elimination over a bounded interface relation is allowed;
enumerating complete interior execution plans to build that relation is not
the intended construction. Preprocessing cannot hide that cost.

Decompose the physical constraint incidence structure into regions with
separators B, |B|<=w; specify bag-size bounds separately if the chosen
decomposition requires them. State exactly how chemical factors, stereo factors,
graph incidences, and actual control obligations are assigned to regions.
If a molecular-graph decomposition does not contain the necessary factor
scopes, report the enlarged width; graph width alone is not a free bound
on the joint problem. A decomposition indexes physical ownership, not
subsets of atoms scheduled to be written in the future.

An annotated region R consists of its physical subgraph, owned constraints,
their current domains, and actual commitments restricted to that region.
Its interface tau exposes boundary vertex/edge ports, relevant local fact
types, open ring obligations, lexical/control phases, and the boundary
attachment of actual active or saved frames. Physical boundary labels and
their equality relations are allowed. Their encoding costs O(log |V|) or
O(log |E|) bits each. Any nonlocal obligation crossing this interface must
be exposed and counted. It cannot be hidden in an unbounded pointer payload.

A compatible outside context C owns disjoint interior vertices, overlaps R
only at identified boundary objects, and agrees on their facts and
interface-exposed commitments. Gluing C[R] identifies those objects, combines
owned constraints once, and connects the actual grammar/return interfaces. Contexts include
all compatible outside physical graphs and actual frame arrangements, not
only contexts observed in tests. Relabelling is allowed only as a consistent
renaming of corresponding boundary ports; retained physical identities are
not merged within an execution.

Parameter convention for this question: D, b, K, and Delta constrain the
region, outside context, and glued configuration. The interface must expose
the boundary facts needed to decide compatibility with those promises
independently of hidden interior details. Width w constrains the region's
separator representation; outside contexts need not have bounded width.
Any extra control parameter h bounds exposed control obligations at this
interface, not the depth of the entire actual stack. Thus the proposed
equivalence uses arbitrary-width outside contexts with matching interface
promises. A theorem quantifying only over bounded-width outside contexts is
a meaningful weaker alternative and must be identified as such. Failure of
an attempted execution gives a false observation, not retrospective exclusion
of its experiment from the common domain.

For each interface type tau, fix a common set of tau-typed outside
experiments independently of the region. An experiment supplies an outside
context compatible with tau and a finite sequence of attempted outside-local
commitments and boundary interactions. Local admissibility is defined by
the event rules independently of Live. The experiment answers true exactly
when at least one labelled execution realizes the prescribed interactions,
allowing hidden interior steps between them, and its resulting state has a
finite accepting completion. An unavailable interaction makes the answer
false; it does not exclude the experiment from comparison. To distinguish
particular successors at the interface, prescribe the tau-visible labelled
action, not merely its emitted text. Define

  R1 ~=_tau R2
    iff every experiment in this common set has the same feasibility
        answer with R1 and R2.

Interior text is existentially hidden in this experiment. The equivalence
does not require two different interiors to emit the same strings: even
different path lengths would make that stronger demand unsuitable. The
physical interior remains in G for later local emissions. When querying a
candidate inside a region, descend/re-root to its owning physical objects,
apply the actual local delta, and recompose; do not infer its outgoing
labelled choices from an anonymous region code alone. A valid construction
must prove that these operations preserve each candidate's Live answer.
This is the bridge from contextual feasibility to exact labelled choices.

Question A: for fixed theta=(w,K,Delta,D,b), possibly extended by an explicitly
justified control parameter h, does ~=_tau have finite index
uniformly over interior size and committed-history length, modulo boundary
renaming? If not, exhibit fixed-parameter regions and compatible contexts
separating arbitrarily many classes. The definition of ~= is a specification,
not a data structure: taking the full continuation set as a summary does
not answer the question.

The number and size of interface types themselves must also be bounded by
the declared parameters, modulo boundary renaming. Putting all interior
history into tau evades this question even if each resulting type has only
one equivalence class. Numeric facts must be in the declared bounded domain
or represented with explicit bit costs; arbitrary values are not a finite
alphabet merely because they are called types.

Question B: can one effectively compute a code sigma(R) in a finite set
S_theta whose equality, for a fixed tau, implies ~=_tau, using explicit
local composition operators? It
need not identify every equivalent pair. Its number of codes and encoding
length must be bounded by stated parameters, not forgotten interior size.
Finite index alone does not give an algorithm or a compact encoding. The
gluing algebra, interface vocabulary, and observation protocol are themselves
deliverables: their adequacy must be established against the event model,
not chosen to make the equivalence theorem trivial.

4. Candidate return-frontier skeleton

The current candidate code combines:

* Boundary ports and residual local incidence orders.
* Actual active/saved-frame commitments and ordered nesting/return links.
* Anonymous interior excursions between interface events, including
  excursions with no remaining physical boundary port.
* Joint chemical/stereo and ring-label domains plus correlated load tuples
  describing occupancy at relevant entry, exit, and internal peaks.

This is a hypothesis, not a proved sufficient statistic. If p is the number
of physical boundary incidences, p<=Delta*|B| for maximum degree Delta.
An O(p) skeleton claim must also account for portless excursions and any
additional control ports. The incidence bound alone bounds neither these
objects, load tuple dimensions, nor contextual equivalence classes.

Correlations are essential. If one realizable excursion has resource tuple
(0,2) and another (2,0), coordinatewise minima invent (0,0). Retain joint
alternatives or prove a safe dominance rule for every compatible context.
Likewise, separate feasible traversal, stereo, and capacity answers do not
establish a jointly feasible serialization.

Parameters must include w, ring capacity K, Delta, D (including stereo
scope/domain bounds), and numeric-value bound b when applicable.
OpenSMILES fixes the available numeric labels
at 100; a proof for K=1 or 2 does not establish practicality at 100. An
abstract generalized K is useful for complexity analysis but does not add
nonstandard labels to MAXIMAL. If interface control obligations require an
extra bound h, prove h is bounded by the advertised parameters or expose h
as a limitation. Setting h equal to all forgotten stack/history is not a
bounded-summary solution. The actual global stack remains legitimate.

Required local operators include introducing a physical object/factor,
joining compatible regions, forgetting a discharged interior object,
updating a local actual commitment, and transferring a return obligation.
Branch composition and inline continuation are different operations. A join
must admit exactly grammar-permitted interleavings and switches. Forgetting
must retain every unresolved consequence before erasing an object's ID.
No operator may enumerate the region's complete future writer executions.

Desired theorem, currently unproved:

  (a) Equality of interface and code implies ~=_tau, uniformly over region
      size and actual-history length, modulo consistent boundary renaming.
  (b) Explicit introduce, join, forget, and actual-commitment operators
      preserve that interpretation and compose to an exact root Live answer.
  (c) Re-rooting/refining to expose a particular interior candidate, applying
      its delta, and recomposing decides Live for that labelled successor.
  (d) Code sizes and operator costs satisfy explicit parameterized bounds,
      including boundary-identifier bits and any numeric-value payload.
  (e) Accepted paths and qualifying standard strings/bijections correspond
      in both directions, and Choices_G is exact at every reachable state.

Boundary renaming in (a) compares abstract region types; it never merges
distinct labelled successors in one execution. None of (a)-(e) follows
merely from local consistency or tests against a sampled writer.

5. Cost questions, separated from correctness

Let N be the input encoding size, M the number of decomposition nodes, S the
mutable state size, c(q) the number of tentative local candidates examined,
and d(q,a) the number of summary nodes whose values must be recomputed for
candidate a. Use theta as above, extended honestly if more parameters
are necessary. Give costs in bits or a stated word-RAM model; large integer
fields and label identities are not free.

Count output as returned labelled successor records plus emitted text bytes
and any independently materialized successor data. Equal text with distinct
states counts multiple times. A handle to a persistent successor counts as
a handle, while allocation, path copying, and retained shared storage are
charged separately. Rejected candidates and propagation are work, not
output. Exhaustion/zero-choice queries also have a cost.

A qualified target is preprocessing P(N,theta), storage
O(N + M*f(theta) + S), and query/update work bounded by

  O(local candidate work
    + sum over examined a of d(q,a)*g(theta)
    + actual successor allocation and output size).

This expression is useful only with independent bounds on preprocessing,
f, g, local candidate work, and dirty-region size. Without those bounds it
merely renames the cost. This is an accounting target, not an established
bound. Specify dependency index construction, decomposition discovery,
tentative updates/rollback,
snapshot retention, and handling of multiple caller-retained successors.
Persistent branching can duplicate update costs; an amortized theorem for
one selected walk does not automatically cover a tree of retained states.

The stronger ambition is small delay per productive successor after
reasonable preprocessing, with total work near actual emitted output plus
unavoidable input and actual-update costs. Can rejected candidates be
avoided or charged to those costs? State worst-case or amortized guarantees,
initial delay, terminal delay, and parameter dependence separately. For an
enumeration API, a useful target form is

  T(q) <= U(N,theta,delta_q) + (1 + B_out(q))*g(theta),

where B_out counts returned labelled records and text, delta_q is the actual
update from a retained predecessor state, and U has an independently proved
maintenance bound. For an initial state use the stated preprocessing bound.
U cannot be defined as whatever complete-future search the implementation
performs. Give costs for a tree of retained successors separately from an
amortized claim along one selected walk.

To make the ambition falsifiable, one strong parameterized target would be

  preprocessing <= N*f(theta)*polylog(2+N),
  storage       <= N*f(theta) + actual retained-state storage,
  T(q)          <= f(theta)*polylog(2+N)*(1 + |delta_q| + B_out(q)).

Here |delta_q| measures the explicitly changed actual records, not all
derived relation nodes subsequently touched. Retained-state storage must
itself be bounded in terms of caller-retained actual states and shared
physical data; it cannot hide an unbounded continuation cache. State the
polynomial and parameter functions. If this target is impossible, identify
the strongest weaker bound and the obstruction. None of these bounds is
claimed established. Reading the input already costs Omega(N); writing ell
bytes costs Omega(ell). A full
copy of the live state is not constant-cost merely because the choice has a
short text label. There is no proposed uniform O(output) theorem here.

Unrestricted separator width must remain in scope as a mathematical case,
even if it prevents a useful uniform bound. Exponential or worse dependence
on w, K, Delta, or stereo domains may defeat the application. A finite
implementation cap, width promise, or chemistry restriction must be named
and cannot silently redefine MAXIMAL. Optional restricted spelling or
RDKit-style profiles may sit above the core; a profile also needs exact
productivity for its restricted language, not arbitrary late filtering.

6. Existing evidence and deliberate falsification

The implementation checkpoint [3] reports that production feasibility still
assigns future ranks, entry points, and branch ends through EventSchedule,
then searches complete compatible futures per candidate. Removing suffix
strings while retaining that planning authority would not meet this goal.
Neither a future-state tree nor an Inside-style DAG indexed by future atom
subsets is the proposed physical separator mechanism.

Test-only boundary-load profiles address a restricted all-Ring model: atoms
are completed in an order and each physical edge is represented by paired
ring events. Live load at a completed-prefix cut equals the number of edges
crossing that cut. Optimizing that maximum is a cutwidth-type subproblem
under the chosen model's order restrictions. This observation is not a
complexity classification of full OpenSMILES. Any claimed hardness transfer
must supply a reduction preserving grammar, rooting, degree, and parameters;
inline edges, branches, and dots may invalidate an informal transfer.

Those profiles and a dirty-ancestor cache have bounded-test agreement with
restricted references. Their changed-node closure can cover the entire
plan. They establish neither the contextual-equivalence theorem nor a small
update bound. The checkpoint motivates retaining actual return-owner and
order information, but does not prove that the richer skeleton is sufficient.

Useful negative results would separate the skeleton by a compatible context;
prove unbounded index for fixed advertised parameters; force large relation
encodings; or force broad updates despite small output. Distinguish a lower
bound for this particular representation from impossibility for every
incremental algorithm. The coupling of ring reuse, nesting, local stereo
order, and unresolved lexical ownership is the main suspected obstacle.

7. Concrete request to experts

Please assess the finite-interface question first. Supply a precise gluing
algebra and sufficient summary with proofs, or the smallest separating
family showing what information is missing. Then address effective
construction, encoding size, and dynamic costs in that order. A restricted
theorem should enumerate its grammar and chemistry omissions and explain
which additional obligations might invalidate its composition argument.
An unrestricted impossibility claim needs explicit assumptions and proof.

The immediate decision is whether the return-frontier approach can replace
global future planning while retaining exact labelled productivity. If it
can, the next deliverable is the minimal joint relation and its operators.
If it cannot, identify whether the obstruction belongs to this representation
or to the broader algorithmic requirements, and suggest a different exact
representation where justified. No implementation change is authorized by
this research note.
